FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Can my friends see my location all the time?

No. Sharing is a separate, explicit choice from following a journey — turning one on never turns the other on for you. You can stop broadcasting with one tap, from the app or straight from the notification.

Does tracking a journey drain my battery?

It uses a single low-priority foreground notification rather than constant background polling, and stops automatically when you arrive, after a few hours, or the moment you swipe the app away.

What happens if I close the app mid-journey?

Swiping MetroSync out of your recent apps ends the journey on purpose — it's the obvious, discoverable way to stop everything: the notification clears and your friends stop seeing you move.

Which networks does it cover?

The Delhi Metro (all lines), the Airport Express, NCR Metro in Gurugram and Noida, and the Delhi–Meerut RRTS — one graph, one app, no switching between line-specific apps.

Is MetroSync available on iPhone?

Not yet — it's Android-only for now. The background tracking that makes live journeys work is being built as an iOS Live Activity next.
